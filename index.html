Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick responses to help clear queries regarding enrollment, syllabus, and schedules.

What are the eligibility criteria for joining?
Most of our core modules (like Tally, DTP, and Python) are open to high school students, college graduates, and corporate professionals. No prior coding experience is required as we start teaching concepts from the very basics.
Do you offer weekday and weekend batches?
Yes, we run both daily weekday batches and consolidated weekend batches. You can work with your class supervisor to customize your lab timetable around existing college or office engagements.
Is the certificate issued by B-Tech valid for jobs?
Absolutely. Our diplomas and specific module certificates are highly recognized by private businesses, local service companies, and accounting agencies around Chinnamanur, Theni, and Tamil Nadu.
Can I pay the program fees in installments?
Yes! To assist students from all backgrounds, we provide installment-based payments, allowing you to split the total fee over easy monthly cycles.
